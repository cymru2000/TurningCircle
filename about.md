TurningCircle is an independent UK site covering the car market and the switch to electric, with researched guides for people who actually buy and run cars.

## What we cover

- New and used car market data
- Electric car buying guides
- Charging networks and running costs
- The stories behind the spec sheets

## How we work

Articles are written by The TurningCircle Team. Every guide is built on researched numbers, and we link to primary sources such as SMMT registration data and GOV.UK policy announcements so you can check our working. We would rather tell you what the data says than what a press release says.
