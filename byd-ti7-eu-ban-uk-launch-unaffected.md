A district court in The Hague has ruled that BYD's Ti7 SUV infringes Jaguar Land Rover's registered EU design for the Defender and has banned the car from sale anywhere in the European Union. The same judgment declined jurisdiction over BYD's UK affiliate, so the British launch of the seven-seat plug-in hybrid is unaffected, with customer deliveries still due in January 2027.

## What the court ordered

On Friday 20 November, the preliminary relief judge at the District Court of The Hague found in case C/09/712003 that the Ti7 infringes the EU design JLR registered for the current Defender, and barred BYD from importing, producing, offering for sale, selling, storing or delivering the car anywhere in the EU ([court notice, 20 November](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/rechtbanken/rechtbank-den-haag/nieuws/2026/11/autofabrikant-byd-mag-suv-model-ti7-niet-in-eu-verhandelen); [Reuters, 20 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-byd-has-suv-that-infringes-jlr-defender-design-dutch-court-rules-2026-11-20/)). The injunction also stops BYD building the Ti7 at its new plant in Szeged, Hungary, which is due to begin mass production early next year, and it takes effect one week after the ruling because BYD has no concrete plans to launch the car in the EU.

The judge compared the Ti7 with the first of six EU designs JLR holds for the Defender L663, filed on 25 April 2018, more than a year before the model reached showrooms in September 2019. He found the bulging shoulders, rounded corners, signature rear panel, rounded wheel-arch cut-outs, continuous window line, proportions and overall silhouette are reflected in the Ti7, and that the differences BYD pointed to were not striking enough to change the overall impression on an informed user.

The ruling is provisional. It came in a kort geding, the Dutch fast-track procedure, and JLR must start full main proceedings within six months of the judgment or the ban loses its force ([court notice](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/rechtbanken/rechtbank-den-haag/nieuws/2026/11/autofabrikant-byd-mag-suv-model-ti7-niet-in-eu-verhandelen)).

## Why Britain is outside the ban

The judge ruled the Dutch court has no jurisdiction over BYD (U.K.) Co. Limited, the company that sells the Ti7 in Britain, so the injunction does not reach the UK launch ([Reuters, 20 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-byd-has-suv-that-infringes-jlr-defender-design-dutch-court-rules-2026-11-20/)). The court also rejected JLR's design claims against the Dutch BYD companies for the UK market, finding JLR had not made it plausible that those entities were involved in offering the Ti7 in Britain ([court notice](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/rechtbanken/rechtbank-den-haag/nieuws/2026/11/autofabrikant-byd-mag-suv-model-ti7-niet-in-eu-verhandelen)). Whether the Ti7 infringes JLR's UK designs remains an open question that only a British court can settle, and JLR is expected to file a UK design case before the January deliveries begin ([CarNewsChina, 21 November](https://carnewschina.com/2026/11/21/dutch-court-blocks-byd-ti7-in-eu-over-defender-design-leaves-uk-launch-untouched/)).

A second Dutch action is already in the calendar. JLR is pursuing copyright infringement and slavish imitation claims against BYD at the District Court of Rotterdam, with the hearing listed for 25 November ([CarNewsChina, 21 November](https://carnewschina.com/2026/11/21/dutch-court-blocks-byd-ti7-in-eu-over-defender-design-leaves-uk-launch-untouched/); [electrive, 14 November](https://electrive.com/2026/11/14/legal-proceedings-should-establish-whether-the-byd-ti7-is-a-copy-of-jlrs-defender)).

## The car and the price gap

The Ti7 is BYD's first seven-seat model for the UK, sold in a single Excellence AWD specification from £47,995 on the road ([BYD UK, 31 July](https://bydukmedia.com/en/news-articles/byd-confirms-pricing-and-specifications-of-its-eagerly-anticipated-first-seven-seat-suv-in-uk-%E2%80%93-ti-7.html)). Its DM-p plug-in hybrid system pairs a 1.5-litre turbocharged petrol engine with three electric motors for a combined 408PS, a 0-62mph time of 4.8 seconds and up to 74 miles of electric-only range from the 35.6kWh Blade battery. Customers can pre-order now with a £500 deposit that BYD matches, and first deliveries are scheduled for January 2027.

Land Rover lists the Defender 110 from £64,315 ([Land Rover UK price list](https://www.landrover.co.uk/defender/defender-110/models-and-specifications.html)), which puts the Ti7 £16,320 below the entry British 4x4 while offering two more seats and a plug. The plug-in Defender range starts higher still, at £71,840 for the P300e.

## What the two companies said

JLR welcomed the outcome. "We protect our intellectual property and take legal action when necessary," the company said in a statement. "Legal proceedings are ongoing, and we won't be commenting further at this time" ([Reuters, 20 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-byd-has-suv-that-infringes-jlr-defender-design-dutch-court-rules-2026-11-20/)).

BYD said it "strongly disagrees" with the ruling and will appeal. "The BYD Ti 7 is the result of BYD's own independent design work and infringes none of JLR's rights," the company said. "We will pursue every legal avenue to demonstrate that this decision is incorrect" ([Reuters, 20 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-byd-has-suv-that-infringes-jlr-defender-design-dutch-court-rules-2026-11-20/)). Filing an appeal does not suspend the EU ban.

## The market behind the case

The Defender is JLR's best-selling model, with global sales of more than 100,000 units in 2025 ([Reuters, 20 November](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-byd-has-suv-that-infringes-jlr-defender-design-dutch-court-rules-2026-11-20/)). JLR's recovery this year has been built on exactly that car, as [our earlier look at its quarterly results](/jlr-quarter-recovery/) noted.

BYD's side of the story is the scale of its European growth. The brand registered 283,997 cars across the EU, UK and EFTA in the first nine months of 2026, up 134.6% on the same period last year and ahead of Ford on 277,269, Tesla on 260,575 and Nissan on 205,387 ([ACEA, 22 October](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_September_2026.pdf)). That momentum is part of the wider shift in the UK market that [we covered when Chinese brands hit a record share](/chinese-brands-record-share/).

This is not the first round of the dispute. In August, bailiffs acting for JLR seized digital records from BYD offices in Schiedam and Hoofddorp, an operation [we covered at the time](/jlr-byd-defender-copycat-case/). On 11 November a different judge in the same court ruled the seizure was unnecessary and ordered JLR to destroy the material ([electrive, 14 November](https://electrive.com/2026/11/14/legal-proceedings-should-establish-whether-the-byd-ti7-is-a-copy-of-jlrs-defender)). Friday's judgment rested on the design comparison and BYD's own launch material, not on the seized files.

## What it means for UK buyers

For anyone ordering a Ti7 in Britain, nothing changes this week. The Dutch injunction does not apply to BYD UK, customer deliveries remain scheduled for January 2027, and the £47,995 price stands. What could still change the picture is a fresh filing in the British courts, which JLR is expected to make before those deliveries begin, or the outcome of the Rotterdam hearing on 25 November. Neither affects the UK order book directly, but both could shape what happens to European supply of the car later.

For buyers weighing the two cars, the arithmetic is unchanged: a seven-seat plug-in hybrid with 74 miles of electric range at £47,995 sits £16,320 below the entry Defender 110, and the court case has not moved either price.
